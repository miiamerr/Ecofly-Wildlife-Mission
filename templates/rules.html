GAME RULES

How to Play

Age Requirement: 12+

  • Choose a destination.
  • Answer the wildlife quiz question correctly.
  • Rescue animals by answering questions.

Scoring

  • Easy: +1 point for a correct answer, -1 for an incorrect answer.
  • Medium: +3 points for a correct answer, -3 for an incorrect answer.
  • Hard: +5 points for a correct answer, -5 for an incorrect answer.

Your Goal

Rescue at least 6 out of 12 animals to win the game!

Be Careful!

Don't run out of fuel or points. Plan your journey carefully!
